Restaurant Services

Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

A commercial kitchen is one of the hardest spaces to keep comfortable. Every burner and fryer adds heat, the exhaust hoods draw conditioned air outside all shift long, and the makeup air system has to replace that air without upsetting the building's pressure. Seaside HVAC services, sizes, and repairs the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep both the kitchen and the dining room comfortable, and we schedule service around your operating hours.

Air balance is where many restaurant HVAC problems start. When a kitchen runs under negative pressure, it pulls dining-room air in the wrong direction, drags hot or cold outdoor air through every door, and wears out your staff at peak service. Our technicians balance the makeup air to the hood exhaust and document every visit in written reports your landlord and auditors can review.

Your schedule comes first. Our team coordinates every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. Off-hours installation is available when needed, and disruption stays minimal so the kitchen keeps running and the dining room stays comfortable.

Safe Installation

Professional Installation and Documentation

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe, comfortable conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can result in carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. Seaside HVAC provides commercial installations and inspections that cover heat exchanger integrity, proper venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge verification, condensate drainage protection, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

Regular professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and acting on expert recommendations keeps minor issues from becoming liabilities. It also creates a record. We keep service logs, temperature and filter records, and a binder of reports for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and auditors that you've done your due diligence.
