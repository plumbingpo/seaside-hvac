Keeping Your Sacramento HVAC System Running Longer

A little routine care is the difference between a system that lasts and one that breaks down in the middle of a heat wave. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: fresh filters every one to three months, at least two feet of open space around the outdoor condenser, a spring rinse of the condenser coil, and a professional tune-up every spring and fall, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

How old is your system? That number says a lot about what comes next. Most well-maintained air conditioners reach 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is past that range and also shows rising bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or frequent repairs, it's usually due for a planned replacement, and that's almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than an emergency replacement when the first heat wave hits.

The sooner you call about a comfort problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A weak capacitor is an easy, low-cost part to replace, yet that same capacitor, neglected for a season, can ruin the compressor and turn a simple fix into a full system replacement. Save our number in your contacts now so you can reach us fast as soon as something sounds or feels wrong in your home. A quick call saves time, money, and sleepless nights.
